A380 for X-Plane 12 - 3D paint kit

1. Open "A380 3D Paint Kit.blend" in Blender 4.2 or newer.
2. Switch to the Texture Paint workspace and paint on any object in:
   - "Exterior - paintable": fuselage, fin, belly, engines, wing fences.
     "Exterior - reference" is the rest of the outside, for context only.
   - "Cabin": seats, carpets, sidewalls, galleys, ceilings, stairs, doors.
   - "Flight deck placards": the registration placard and the cockpit
     stickers.
3. The IFE look: open textures/ife/ife_logo.png (transparent, shown on the
   welcome screen) and textures/ife/ife_background.png in the Image Editor
   and paint them, or replace the files with your own 512 x 256 images.
4. Cabin lighting and IFE colours: edit the "cabin.json" text in the
   Scripting workspace.
   - scenes: boarding, taxi, takeoff, climb, cruise, meal, night, sunrise,
     descent, landing, arrival. Each has a colour and a level from 0 to 1.
     A colour is a hex code like "#7b3fd0" or one of white, warm, amber,
     pink, purple, blue, teal, night. The cabin light strips use the nearest
     of those eight; the light falling on the cabin uses the exact colour.
   - stars: true puts the starry ceiling in first class for the night scene.
   - ife: airline name, welcome text, and colours for the IFE screens.
5. Image > Save All Images now and then.
6. Open the "export_livery.py" text in the Scripting workspace, set
   LIVERY_NAME at the top and press Run Script. It writes every exterior
   sheet, only the cabin and flight deck sheets you changed, the IFE images
   and cabin.json.
7. Copy the new folder from liveries/ into
   X-Plane 12/Aircraft/A380 for X-Plane 12/liveries/ and pick it in X-Plane.

In flight the cabin scenes follow the flight on their own. To pick a scene or
set the cabin brightness yourself, use Cabin lighting in the XPHFBW settings
(X-Plane's aircraft menu). The seatback screens show the IFE in economy and
in first class.
