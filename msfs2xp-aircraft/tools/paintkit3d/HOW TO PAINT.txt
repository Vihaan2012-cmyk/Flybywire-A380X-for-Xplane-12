A380 for X-Plane 12 - 3D paint kit

1. Open "A380 3D Paint Kit.blend" in Blender 4.2 or newer.
2. Switch to the Texture Paint workspace. Pick an object in the "Paintable"
   collection (fuselage, fin, belly, engines, wing fences) and paint on it.
   "Reference" holds the rest of the aircraft for context; it is not exported.
3. Image > Save All Images now and then. The paintable sheets are the PNGs in
   textures/paint.
4. Open the Scripting workspace, open the "export_livery.py" text, set
   LIVERY_NAME at the top and press Run Script.
5. Copy the new folder from liveries/ into
   X-Plane 12/Aircraft/A380 for X-Plane 12/liveries/ and pick it in X-Plane.
