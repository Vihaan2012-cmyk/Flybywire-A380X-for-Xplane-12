# TLD GPU-409-E-CUP, 90 kVA diesel ground power unit

## Sources

- TLD dimensioned drawing in the Aerospecialties gallery: `TLD_GPU-409-E-CUP_90kva_2.jpg`.
- Product photos: Aerospecialties gallery (4), and Aeroservicios stock 6448, a 2015 unit with serial T36297 (12).
- Spec text, which Aerospecialties and Fortbrand agree on: 112″ × 68″ × 59″ (284 × 173 × 148 cm). The spec also lists:
  - rubber torsion axle and fifth-wheel steering;
  - no-drop towbar brake;
  - forklift pockets and tie-down rings;
  - side cable trays with integral rollers, and cable plug holders;
  - optional second 400 Hz output and amber beacon.
- Aircraft side: Airbus A380 AC section 5-4-4.
  - External power is behind access doors 133AL and 134AR, 5.99 m aft of the nose, 0.45 m left or right of the centreline, mean height 2.59 m.
  - There are four MS 90362-3 receptacles, 90 kVA.

Photos are kept in `D:/A380/gse-refs/gpu_tld409` and never ship.

## Frame

- Units are millimetres.
- *x*: from the chassis rear end (the control-panel end) towards the towbar.
- *y*: from the centreline, positive to the right when looking from the towbar end.
- *z*: up from the ground.
- Drawing scale: 2.566 mm per pixel on the 4× enlarged side view, 2.552 on the end view. Values marked **(d)** are measured off the drawing; values marked **(p)** are estimated from photos.

## Connection (right side, rear corner)

- Plug holder: a white moulded box with a black recessed socket, on the enclosure side at x ≈ 150–330, z ≈ 1150–1400 (p).
- Cable gland at the bottom of the rear side panel, x ≈ 250, z ≈ 800. There are two outputs: the standard one and an optional second (p).
- 400 Hz cable: black rubber, Ø ≈ 45 (p). It runs down into the right-side cable tray and lies coiled there (p).
- Plug: MS 90362 six-pin 400 Hz plug.
  - Black rubber body Ø ≈ 110, length ≈ 260, with a moulded grip and a yellow interlock ring (p).
  - In use, plugged into the receptacle behind door 134AR at 2.59 m.

## Chassis (hot-dip galvanized)

- Centre box under the enclosure, 1114 wide, from z 203 up to z 752 (d).
- Side cable trays, one each side (d, p):
  - outer edge at y ±862, about 300 wide;
  - top at z 620, bottom at z 400;
  - the outer wall slopes outwards about 10°;
  - a Ø 42 tube rail runs along the top outer edge, from the rear end to x ≈ 2300, where the tray angles inboard towards the front wheel.
- Rear end: galvanized end plate below the enclosure, with "TLD" stamped on each tray end, red reflectors and tie-down rings (p).
- Front: fifth-wheel turntable above the front axle; the towbar pivots at about (x 2510, z 240) (d).
- Towbar: galvanized, held upright by its no-drop brake. Its top eye ring is at z ≈ 1550, with a red and white chevron band on the upright (d, p).
