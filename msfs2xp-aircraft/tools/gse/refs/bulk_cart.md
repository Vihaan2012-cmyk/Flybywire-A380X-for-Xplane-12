- No AVIOGEI, TUG or Fast Global Solutions sheet found, and no photos downloaded (E:/fbw-gse/refs/bulk_cart is empty). Roof arch, curtain layout and wheel size are **(p)**, estimated from memory of typical carts.

## Frame

- Units are millimetres, as in the GPU sheet.
- *x*: from the rear hitch end towards the drawbar. Model origin at x 2000.
- *y*: positive to the right when looking from the drawbar end. The rolled-up curtain is on +y, the closed curtain on -y.
- *z*: up from the ground.
