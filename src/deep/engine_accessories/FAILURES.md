# Failures — engine_accessories

One row per genuinely distinct physical fault. `ATA | proposed name | model element it acts on | magnitude meaning (0..1) | effect`. All ×4 for the four engines (the plugin multiplies).

| ATA | proposed name | model element | magnitude (0..1) | effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 73 | LP fuel pump wear | `fuel::lp_pump::LpPumpFaults.wear` | 0 healthy .. 1 seized | Derates the centrifugal pressure-rise coefficient; less boost pressure to the HP pump inlet, worsening cavitation margin. |
| 73 | LP fuel pump inlet restriction | `fuel::lp_pump::LpPumpFaults.inlet_restriction` | 0 clear .. 1 blocked | Throttles suction pressure seen by the pump; combined with hot fuel, drives NPSH below required and cavitates (flow and pressure both fall). |
| 73 | Fuel filter clog | `fuel::filter::FilterFaults.clog` | 0 clean .. 1 blocked | Differential pressure rises as `1/(1-clog)^2` until the bypass valve cracks at 35 psi; beyond that, unfiltered fuel reaches the HP pump. |
| 73 | HP fuel pump wear | `fuel::hp_pump::HpPumpFaults.wear` | 0 healthy .. 1 worn | Internal (gear-clearance) slip flow grows as `1/(1-wear)^2` at a given discharge pressure; delivered flow falls short of the theoretical displacement flow. |
| 73 | HP fuel pump inlet starvation | `fuel::hp_pump::HpPumpFaults.inlet_starvation` | 0 none .. 1 total | Caps delivered flow directly (fuel simply is not there to fill the gear pockets), fed forward from an upstream LP pump/cavitation shortfall. |
| 73 | FMU metering valve sticking | `fuel::fmu::FmuFaults.valve_sticking` | 0 free .. 1 seized | Scales the valve actuator's slew rate to zero; the valve tracks the commanded flow ever more slowly and, fully stuck, freezes at whatever position it was in. |
| 73 | FMU spill valve stuck open | `fuel::fmu::FmuFaults.spill_stuck_open` | 0 healthy .. 1 fully open | Collapses the regulated differential across the metering valve toward zero; metered flow starves even at full valve area (risk: flameout). |

| 73 | HP fuel shut-off valve stuck | `fuel::shutoff_valve::ShutoffValveFaults.stuck` | 0 free .. 1 seized | Scales travel rate to zero; a valve stuck open cannot shut an engine down on a fire-handle pull, one stuck closed flames the engine out and blocks a restart. |

| 73 | Fuel flow transmitter pickoff frozen (×2 channels A/B) | `fuel::flow_transmitter::PickoffFaults.frozen` | 0 live .. 1 fully frozen | Blends the channel's output toward its last held value; fully frozen it stops updating altogether while the other channel keeps tracking. |

| 74 | Igniter plug erosion (×2 chains A/B) | `ignition::IgnitionFaults.igniter_a_erosion`/`igniter_b_erosion` | 0 new .. 1 fully eroded | Raises the gap's breakdown voltage; once it exceeds the exciter's fixed peak output, that chain stops firing outright (a hard cutoff, not a gradual derate). |
| 80 | Starter air valve stuck | `starting::air_valve::AirValveFaults.stuck` | 0 free .. 1 seized | Travel rate to zero; reads as stuck-open (keeps driving the starter past normal handoff) or stuck-closed (no start air) depending on the commanded direction when it seized. |
| 80 | Starter clutch fails to engage | `starting::turbine::AtsFaults.clutch_fails_to_engage` | 0 full .. 1 none | Reduces torque transmitted to the spool during cranking; a hung start with the turbine itself spinning normally. |
| 80 | Starter clutch fails to disengage | `starting::turbine::AtsFaults.clutch_fails_to_disengage` | 0 healthy .. 1 fully coupled | Couples spool speed into the starter rotor past its own free speed, dragging the spool; combined with a sustained high N3 (e.g. the FADEC's own overspeed setpoint), forces the rotor past its structural limit and disintegrates it. |
| 72 | IP compressor VSV actuator jam | `airflow_control::vsv::VsvFaults.jam` | 0 free .. 1 seized | Freezes the vane ring; the growing schedule error costs stall margin (`VsvState.stall_margin_delta_pct`), a documented output for the compressor model to apply. |
| 72 | IP compressor VSV rigging error | `airflow_control::vsv::VsvFaults.rigging_bias_deg` | signed degrees, 0 = none | A healthy, fully responsive actuator still settles off the true schedule by the bias amount, costing stall margin exactly as a jam does. |
| 75 | IP handling bleed valve jam | `airflow_control::bleed_valve::BleedValveFaults.jam` (IP) | 0 free .. 1 seized | Jammed open at high power bleeds core air the engine needs; jammed closed at low power loses the stall margin the valve exists to provide. |
| 75 | HP handling bleed valve jam | `airflow_control::bleed_valve::BleedValveFaults.jam` (HP) | 0 free .. 1 seized | Same as the IP handling bleed, on the HP-stage valve, which closes later in the schedule. |
