# Flight model — failures

Plain-table version of every failure this model's fault inputs support (the
structured, code-registered version the lead's `Registry` consumes is
`registry.rs`; the two are kept in sync by construction — this table was
generated by hand from the same list `registry.rs` builds).

`ATA | proposed name | model element it acts on | magnitude meaning (0..1) | effect`

## Environment/airframe aerodynamics (ATA 30/57) — `aerodynamics.rs`

| ATA | name | model element | magnitude (0..1) | effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | Wing ice accretion | `AeroFaults.wing_ice_fraction` | 0=clean..1=severe | CLmax down to 50%, lift-curve slope down to 60%, parasite drag up; higher stall speed, earlier/harder stall |
| 30 | Tailplane ice accretion | `AeroFaults.tail_ice_fraction` | 0=clean..1=severe | tail CLmax/lift-curve slope reduced; the classic tailplane-icing hazard (reduced/uncommanded pitch authority, worse with flaps) |
| 57 | Airframe aerodynamic damage | `AeroFaults.airframe_damage_fraction` | 0=undamaged..1=severe | CLmax down to 70%, lift-curve slope down to 80%, parasite drag up to +80% |

Not a failure (an environmental/operational *input*, not a fault): runway
contamination (`GearInputs.runway_mu_factor`, 1.0=dry down to ~0.05=ice) and
wind/gusts/shear (`FlightModelInputs.wind`) — these vary normal operations,
they do not represent a broken aircraft, so they are not registered as
failures in `registry.rs` (consistent with the brief's "genuinely distinct
physical fault" guidance: a wet runway is not a fault of the aircraft).
