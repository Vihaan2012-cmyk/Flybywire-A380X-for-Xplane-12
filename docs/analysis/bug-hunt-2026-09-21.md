# Bug hunt, 2026-09-21

Ten agents, one hour each, one area each, reading the whole tree and fixing
only inside the files they owned; two more on known defects. Everything
they fixed is merged into `main` (`scan/01` … `scan/10`, plus the direct
commits named below). Everything they found and could not fix is here, so
the next pass starts from evidence rather than from a fresh scan.

The rule for the hunt was the project's own: a failure produces its effect
through the physics, never a scripted shortcut, and no value is invented.
Several agents came back with nothing in an hour, and said so. That is a
result: those areas (`sensors`, `wiring`, `cabin`, `pneumatic_ducts`, the
ECAM wiring layer, `plugin.rs`'s Truth builder, `fuel_network.rs`) had
already been through a hardening pass and their `PROGRESS.md` files record
it. The findings below cluster where that pass had not reached.

## Verified live, same day

X-Plane relaunched with the merged plugin, the reconverted aircraft and
the powered preset driven over X-Plane's UDP dataref port
(`fbw/A32NX_AIRCRAFT_PRESET_LOAD = 2`). What the run settled:

| Question | Result |
| --- | --- |
| White rectangles on the glareshield and overhead | Gone. The validator-placeholder fix in the converter was the cause; the gloss theory was not. |
| OIT views 15/16 failing on `process is not defined` | Log count 0 (was 8). |
| Displays "text but no vector geometry" | **Does not reproduce on a powered aircraft.** PFD attitude sphere, pitch ladder, tapes, ND compass rose, E/WD arcs, ISIS all draw. The earlier screens were a half-powered aircraft in self-test -- the same pushbutton root cause as the dead aircraft. `glGetError` after every upload: silent. |
| Overhead seeding | AC 1, AC ESS and DC ESS buses energised, APU 100 %, generator normal, within a minute of the preset. |
| Plugin cost | 36 frames/s, 5.96 ms per frame. |

Two new defects surfaced in the invariant log and were fixed the same
session: `NW_STEER_ANGLE_DEG`/`BODY_STEER_ANGLE_DEG` were NaN every frame
(the shimmy integrator's explicit damping step is divergent below ~28
m/s, i.e. on every parked aircraft -- now implicit); and
`ENGINE_CREEP_LIFE_FRACTION` 1.3-1.8 on all four engines was clamped to
1 by the generic FRACTION rule although the damage model counts past 1
by design.

Two things it also showed, unfixed:

- `Truth.controls.steering_command_deg` is never written by the Truth
  builder -- only tests set it. The deep steering model runs with a
  permanently zero command; the tiller and rudder pedals never reach it.
- The ND shows GS 29 on a parked aircraft after the preset, most likely
  IRs not aligned; not investigated.
- The persisted creep of 1.3-1.8 was accumulated in 0.07 engine-hours
  across 3 cycles (`Output/preferences/fbw_a380x_airframe.json`). Miner's
  rule at the 20 s overtemperature budget makes that possible only if
  EGT sat above the overtemperature limit for ~30 s per engine; whether
  that happened or the EGT fed to `physics::damage` was wrong in an
  earlier session is not known. Worth watching `A32NX_ENGINE_EGT` against
  the damage model's input on the next engine start.

## Found, not fixed -- in order of what a pilot would notice

### Armed failures with no consumer

The largest single class, and the one the project's brief is about.

- **36 of 76 per-engine failure ids** (`src/failures.rs::extra::engines()`,
  ATA 72-80) write `FAIL_ENGINE_COMPONENT_HOOK` and nothing reads it:
  FADEC channel fault, fuel metering valve stuck, igniter fault, throttle
  resolver fault, EGT/N1/oil-pressure/fuel-flow sensor faults, reverser
  lock fault, starter valve stuck. Arming "Engine 2 N1 tachometer fault"
  from the Study panel changes nothing. The other 32 (bearing wear,
  compressor stall, blade damage, the exotic modes, oil pump, oil leak)
  are consumed through `engine_commands.rs`. Wiring the 36 means sensor
  tap points in `engine_commands.rs`/`fadec.rs`, a design decision, not a
  patch.
- Seven more hook families with the same shape, by grep, unverified
  individually: `FAIL_ADIRU_SENSOR_HOOK` (9 ids), `FAIL_ADIRU_INTERNAL_HOOK`
  (3), `FAIL_BUS_SHORT_HOOK` (18), `FAIL_HYDRAULIC_HOOK` (6),
  `FAIL_FUEL_HOOK` (47 -- a comment at `fuel_network.rs:846` may mean these
  are wired; check), `FAIL_BLEED_DUCT_LEAK_HOOK`/`FAIL_PRECOOLER_HOOK` (8;
  `physics/air.rs` is 48 lines and calls itself a placeholder),
  `FAIL_APU_FUEL_CONTROL_HOOK`/`FAIL_APU_STARTER_HOOK` (2).
- **Per-computer PRIM/SEC failures** (`src/prim.rs`, ids 27_000-27_005)
  are never read by `deep::flight_controls`, whose `ComputerHealth` treats
  all six as healthy whenever any AC power exists. Its own comment says
  "Truth names no per-computer bus". Needs a per-computer field on `Truth`.

### Consequences computed and dropped

- **`deep/integration/xp_consequences.rs`** (ice drag, bird-strike
  leading-edge drag, a collapsed gear leg) has zero live callers. All three
  producers were checked and do emit nonzero deltas under the right
  conditions (`icing.rs::thicker_ice_costs_more_lift_and_drag`;
  `bird_strike.rs:258`, algebraically nonzero but with no direct test;
  `strut.rs:711/723`, `gear_structure/mod.rs:560/709`). The frame loop
  needs one `XpForceSink` at start and a per-tick conversion of each
  area's outputs; drag must become force as q·S·ΔCd with S = 845 m².
- **`deep/integration/flight_control_surfaces.rs`** -- the flight-controls
  analogue -- also has zero external callers. Same wiring pass.
- **`extra_backend_fbw.rs::apply_reverser_thrust`** (lines 281-298) writes
  `LOCAL_VX`/`LOCAL_VZ`/`YAW_RATE` directly instead of applying a force.
  The clearest scripted effect left in the tree. Fix shape: the plug-force
  datarefs every flight-loop callback, with the magnitude from FlyByWire's
  own thrust model rather than back-solved from a velocity delta. Needs a
  running sim to verify.

### Smaller

- `physics/engine/mod.rs:641` floors `dt` at 1e-4 -- the shape that moved
  the APU rotor on zero-duration ticks; check whether it matters here.
- `deep_ecam_bridge.js` steps the confirm/shown timers on `Date.now()`
  rather than `_deltaTime`. `docs/deep/ecam_bridge.md` records this as a
  deliberate workaround for `_deltaTime` reading NaN and stalling the FWS;
  whether that failure is still live was not established. Do not change
  without establishing it.
- `src/fuel.rs:164` and `src/fuel_network.rs:124` both define
  `JET_A_LBS_PER_GAL`, 6.699 and 6.7, neither cited precisely enough to
  prefer. Harmless today because `fuel.rs:570` overrides the network's;
  drifts silently if that call moves.
- `OVHD_ELEC_BAT_1/2_PB_IS_AUTO` are seeded at four separate call sites
  rather than in `OVERHEAD_DEFAULTS_ON`. Every path checked does seed
  before first read; a maintainability risk, not a live bug.
- `xphfbw.forceCpuRendering` still has no consumer. Wiring it means
  promoting `display/soft.rs` (test-only today, and correct) into a live
  path with a texture upload and a per-frame cost -- a feature, not a fix.
- The fixture's `/vars` is empty by design (`dump_fixture` refuses to
  invent readings). Consequence: the EFB harness can verify layout, never
  live-value rendering. A clearly-labelled synthetic test fixture would
  close that honestly.
